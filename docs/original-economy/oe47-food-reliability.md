The source-level cursor distinction is narrow and quantified. Complete saved

**Fire, operation and reachability, F/G.** Fire contributes before and after

operation, occupancy and fire are checked again at actual arrival.

Both distributors retain nonnull routes to every House, road revision580 stays

not counted as an executed control or gameplay result.

This is a bounded legal-control search, not proof that every possible paid plan
fails. The reproduced physical selection gaps and six unsuccessful joint
controls justify considering a separately selected contract for **Market→House
Food selection only**. They do not justify changing Farm→Market transport,
production, crop quality, consumption, arrivals, fire, costs or capacity limits.
Historical Rule17/schema33 and older behavior must remain exact; a proposed
selector and technical tests alone establish no paid improvement.
