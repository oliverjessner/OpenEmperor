## Separate Rule16 contract and dispatcher

After the reproduced baseline, concrete dispatch bottleneck and first three fresh legal control failures established the case, a separate explicit **City-v16 Rule16 / Policy3 / Schema32** contract was added. Implementation began while the remaining controls continued on the frozen Rule15 binary; all12 historical controls completed before the final Rule16 acceptance gates.

Rule16 uses existing World fire risk, real protection expiry and prepared Navigator routes. It computes new-dispatch outward ETA from the prepared edge count of an idle actor, and incoming ETA from remaining edges and actual progress, then predicts the unchanged placement-relative ignition time. All paid building classes use one physical deadline ranking. Feasible arrivals outrank already late candidates; the initial pivot has least feasible arrival laxity, while the late fallback has greatest lateness. Ties use deadline, physical round trip and the existing canonical cursor. There is no Elite priority or unconditional burning-first class. A real moving matching Inspector coalesces another dispatch when its ETA meets the target deadline or is no later than the candidate; returning, waiting, disabled or pending actors do not create promised protection.

One bounded linear scan may select an earlier-deadline alternate `k` before that initial pivot only if `k`'s actual outward trip and full physical return leave the pivot able to meet its original deadline, while pivot-first would make `k` miss its deadline. Burning response must arrive strictly before natural expiry; movement on an ignition tick can still precede that tick's fire step. The exchange dispatches only the immediate visit and reevaluates after real return. It creates no saved plan, all-pairs graph, extra Navigator, second future visit, protection grant or mutable authority.
